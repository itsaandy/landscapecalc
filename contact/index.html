Report a problem

Use the feedback form to report a formula, coefficient, label, source or accessibility issue. The form asks only for your email address and feedback; the current LandscapeCalc page is added automatically.

Open the feedback form

What to include

  • The page or calculator you used.
  • The selected material and shape.
  • The dimensions and depth entered.
  • The displayed result and what you expected instead.
  • A current supplier or manufacturer link if you are correcting a product conversion.

Before ordering

LandscapeCalc provides planning estimates and cannot confirm a supplier's delivered volume, product density, installation requirement or vehicle load. Check the Methodology & Sources and confirm the selected product with the supplier before purchase.
