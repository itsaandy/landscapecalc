Privacy Policy

How LandscapeCalc handles information, analytics, advertising, and cookies.

Effective date: 16 August 2026

Calculator information

Calculator inputs are processed in your browser and are not submitted to a LandscapeCalc application server. If you use the share feature, the selected calculator settings may be included in the shared URL.

Analytics

LandscapeCalc uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics may collect information such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser information, referring pages, and interaction events. This information helps us maintain and improve the calculators.

You can learn how Google handles information from sites that use its services in Google's partner sites privacy information. You can also use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising and cookies

LandscapeCalc uses Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's previous visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to LandscapeCalc and other sites on the internet.

You can manage or opt out of personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some site or advertising features.

Consent and privacy choices

Where required, LandscapeCalc may display a consent or privacy message that lets visitors consent, decline, or manage advertising and analytics choices. Available choices depend on the visitor's location and applicable requirements.

External links

This site may link to external websites. LandscapeCalc is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site, its service providers, or applicable requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
