Volume: Length × Width × Depth (converted to metres). For circles: π × radius² × depth. For triangles: ½ × base × height × depth.

Weight: Volume × the selected indicative density. Confirm the exact product conversion with the supplier; see Methodology & Sources.

20 kg equivalent: Estimated kilograms ÷ 20, rounded up. This is a mass comparison, not a retail pack count.

1 m³ bulk-bag equivalent: Volume ÷ 1.0, rounded up. Confirm the supplier's actual bulk-bag size.

Choose the Product Before Converting to Weight

Washed, brickies, paving and sandpit products are not interchangeable. Selecting a subtype changes the indicative density used for the secondary weight result; it does not decide which product is suitable.

Moisture and Grading Matter

Bulk sand weight changes with moisture, mineral composition, grading and compaction. Use cubic metres for the initial volume calculation, then confirm the exact product conversion with the supplier.

Pack Sizes Vary

The 20 kg output is a mass equivalent rather than a retail bag count. Check the bag's stated mass or litre volume and coverage before purchase. All formulas, coefficients and rounding rules are published on the Methodology & Sources page.
