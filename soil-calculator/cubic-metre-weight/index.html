What the Soil Weight Result Means

The page starts with a 1m × 1m area at 1,000mm depth, which is exactly one cubic metre. With topsoil selected, LandscapeCalc multiplies that volume by its 1.10 tonnes-per-cubic-metre planning coefficient to display 1.10 tonnes, or 1,100 kilograms.

It Is a Planning Coefficient, Not a Product Specification

Soil density changes with composition, screening, organic content, moisture and compaction. For comparison, Australian Native Landscapes publishes indicative loose values of 1.4 tonnes per cubic metre for topsoil and 1.2 for garden mix, while warning that actual products vary. LandscapeCalc's complete coefficient list and source policy are documented on the Methodology & Sources page.

How to Use the Estimate

Order bulk soil primarily from the calculated cubic metres. Use the weight only for an early delivery discussion, then replace it with the selected supplier's current conversion. Do not use this estimate to decide whether a vehicle or trailer can legally or safely carry the load.

For a different area or depth, use the main soil calculator and enter the dimensions from your project requirements.
